The [[Player]] takes an elevator up without being noticed. This should let the [[Player]] chill in the arrived room without any danger so they can think about their escape.

There should be a locked door and a large glass window from which a larger area is seen.
Inside the large area, [[Magic]] is to be shown and that it can be used in both offensive and defensive manners. 

This is done by having two robots play fighting each other - one casting a [[Shield]] and the other casting [[Fire Ball]]s. All of the [[Fire Ball]]s are to be blocked by the [[Shield]]. After a bit of time, the robots should run out of mana, pick up nearby [[Mana Gib]]s and go back to play fighting.

---

After they playfight for a while, the [[Player]] should get spotted and one of the robots shoots a [[Fire Ball]] at the large glass window, breaking it.

The two robots then run away in a direction that has a door. This door is unlocked initially but locked afterwards so the player can not go past it.

---

Since the large window is now broken, the [[Player]] can jump out of it into the large room.

Inside this large room the [[Player]] should see various resources used to train [[Magic]]:
[[Mana Gib]]s, [[Health Gib]]s, Targets and a [[Spell Container]]. Picking up the [[Spell Container]] grants the [[Player]] the [[Fire Ball]] spell and shows a hint in the corner of the screen indicating the use of the spell, alongside the spellbook keybind.

---

The [[Player]] is expected to try casting and firing the spells, destroying a few targets. After destroying some of the targets, the locked door opens and one of the robots that was play fighting earlier comes back and starts attacking the [[Player]].

The [[Player]] now must use the newly learnt [[Fire Ball]] spell to kill the robot before it kills the [[Player]].
