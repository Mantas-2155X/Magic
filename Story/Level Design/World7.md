[[Player]] spawns inside a 60degree tube and takes a second to fall into a room. This should be a relatively small room with a light and a hole in the wall to exit it. Also, a hole in the ceiling from which the player dropped down. To show off object interaction, the hole in the wall should be blocked by (breakable) boxes that can be pushed or destroyed.

At that point the player goes through a hallway with multiple locked doors on both sides and one unlocked door. 

A [[Fire Breather]] is standing behind the unlocked door on the left and attacks [[Player]] as soon as the door opens. The room behind the door should be a storage-shed like room with [[Health Canister]]s and, [[Mana Canister]]s on (unbreakable) boxes.

In the hallway on the right there is a hole in the wall but it is too high to jump into so you need to move boxes over and jump on them to get there. The [[Player]] should figure this out hopefully and proceed to jump in there.

After boosting and jumping the [[Player]] falls into a large room. This room should kinda be a production lab of [[Torus]] and should have a few large glass tube containers with some of them inside. 

These tubes are destroyable so if they're shot, the [[Torus]] escape and attack the [[Player]]. In the middle of the room, a small control panel exists with a [[Button]], that when pressed will spawn [[Torus]] into the (non broken) tubes.

The room where all this is happening should have one locked door blocking the [[Player]] from leaving. Assuming the [[Player]] cares to play around, after destroying an amount of [[Torus]], a [[Fire Breather]] will enter the room and attack the [[Player]], therefore unlocking the door and allowing the [[Player]] to leave.

![[world7_2.png]]

After the [[Player]] leaves the room he enters a dimly lit large spacious area with a bridge with a railing to cross over to the other side. This bridge should be partially broken and have gaps the [[Player]] needs to jump over. 

If you fall, you drop into the [[Void]] which kills you. 

Midway while traveling the bridge, a [[Drone]] should deploy in the [[Player]]s view and attack him. After destroying the [[Drone]], multiple [[Torus]] should spawn from behind and chase after the [[Player]]. When the end of the bridge is reached a door is visible and a [[Torus Summoner]] walks in and spawns [[Torus]]es, attacks the player.
