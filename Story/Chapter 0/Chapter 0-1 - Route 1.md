The [[Player]] gets caught walking around by [[Worker]]s. Since they can't attack, they should indicate communication that a [[Defective]] robot was spotted outside of the [[Disassembler]] room. This could be a light, sound or any other means.

---

Alarm lights or sounds will start playing seconds after, with an [[Incinerator]] [[Warp]]ing next to the [[Player]] (in visible view), casting a spell and destroying the [[Player]]. This can be a smoother transition by showing the [[Incinerator]] casting a spell and the screen fading black.
