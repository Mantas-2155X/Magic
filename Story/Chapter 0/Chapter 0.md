The [[Player]] spawns in an assembly line with multiple robots in front and behind.
Assembly line sequence goes on for a few minutes maximum to not bore the [[Player]].

[[Player]] is allowed to look around but can not move. Assembly line has glass walls from which the [[Player]] can see [[Worker]]s inspecting the line process, making other robots, walking around, idling.

---

Eventually the assembly line gets to a scanning checkpoint where the robots are scanned for defects. Some of the robots in front are [[Defective]] (indicated by scanner showing red) and sent to a different direction from [[Valid]] robots (indicated by showing green). 

[[Player]] is also sent to the defective line and must see this differentiation. Either turn the view forcefully or play sounds/lights to draw attention.

---

Finishing up the assembly line, robots are slowly let off the assembly line after being inspected by multiple [[Worker]]s and indicated that they should move to the [[Disassembler]] by either pointing somewhere or showing lights or showing a color indicating what color path they should take.

The [[Player]] is now allowed to move and is expected to follow the path of the other robots from the same line.

[[Death]] will respawn the player at this location.

---

[[Defective]] robots follow the instructions and walk to the [[Disassembler]] where they are destroyed by falling into a hole filled with glowing red liquid. If the [[Player]] chooses to follow the same path, they must see the destruction and can also get disassembled if they wish to do so.

At this point the [[Player]] should know that their sole purpose of walking there was to be destroyed. Sneaking out of the [[Disassembler]] room is now the only way the [[Player]] can go to proceed further in the game.

If the [[Player]] chooses to stay in the [[Disassembler]] room, every some time there will be more [[Defective]] robots walking in to get destroyed to show that it is a common occurance.

---
